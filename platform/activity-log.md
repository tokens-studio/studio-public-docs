# Activity Log

The activity log allows users to see the events and actions completed within an organization.

This may be useful for teams who have legal and compliance requirements around security, or teams that want to manage records of access and settings.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/2025-07-30 at 12.38.17 - Screengrab@2x.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

## Accessing the Activity Log

You can access the activity log by clicking on the **Legal & Compliance** link in the main sidebar.

Once within, you can select the **Compliance Log** tab.

In the Compliance Log tab, you'll see a list of events that detail the actions users have taken in your organization. Some examples include creating subscription updates, user role management and more.&#x20;
